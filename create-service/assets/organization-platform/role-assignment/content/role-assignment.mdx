# Role assignment

Role assignment lets a Tenant Admin say which Xtremax people get which role in
their tenant, based on who they are in the Organisation Platform: their job
title, unit, name and any other attribute it holds. After that, people are
placed automatically when they sign in, and their roles follow their profile
when it changes. Nobody has to invite them.

<Callout type="info">
  You need to be a Tenant Admin of the tenant you are in. The **Role
  assignment** entry appears in the sidebar under **Automation**.
</Callout>

## How a rule works

A rule says: *people who match these conditions get this role in this tenant.*

- **Include** the people who match (for example, designation is *Product
  Manager*), and optionally **exclude** others (for example, people who sign in
  with email and password).
- Conditions can use the Organisation Platform's own attributes, any custom
  attributes it defines, and two things getFeedback knows: **Email domain** and
  **Sign-in method**.
- Choose **Tenant Member** or **Tenant Admin**. These are the only two roles a
  rule can give.
- Rules run **top to bottom for each job a person holds, and the first match
  wins**. If a person matches different jobs, they get the strongest role.
  Use the arrows on the list to change the order, and the switch to turn a
  rule off.

Use **Test rules** to look up a real person by email and see which rule would
decide for each of their jobs. Testing changes nothing.

## When people are placed

When someone signs in, getFeedback asks the Organisation Platform once who they
are, runs the rules of every tenant, and sets their role in each tenant whose
rules match. One person can end up in several tenants. If the Organisation
Platform is slow or down, nothing changes, and the next page load tries again.

## What rules never do

- **Unclear never means remove.** If a tenant has no rules, a rule is broken, or
  no rule covers a person, nothing is granted and nothing is taken away.
- **People you placed by hand keep their role.** Whoever created the tenant, and
  anyone invited or edited on the **Members** page, is never changed by rules.
  Rules only change roles that rules created.
- **A tenant always keeps an admin.** Rules never remove or demote the last
  Tenant Admin.
- **Rules only affect your tenant**, and can never give the platform-wide
  Administrator role.
